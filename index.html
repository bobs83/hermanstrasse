About the apartment

Bright, fully furnished top-floor apartment, a stone's throw from Schillerkiez, one of Berlin's trendier neighbourhoods.

Facing a quiet inner courtyard, you're steps from a pharmacy, Penny, DM, REWE, and parcel shops - everything you need within minutes. The U8 and Ringbahn are right on your doorstep, connecting you to Friedrichshain, Schöneberg, and beyond in minutes.

54 m² + 8 m² terrace spread across an open-plan living and kitchen area plus two bedrooms, with warm oak-style floors, charming sloped ceilings, and a stylish dark-green kitchen.

Step outside onto your private terrace and take in views over lush tree canopies and a historic church tower. Perfect for a couple, two friends, or a solo professional.

The apartment has a great energy and calm. Just bring your bags and enjoy a Berlin summer from your own terrace.
